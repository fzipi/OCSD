# Objectives for Module 11 - Business Logic Security

This module covers flaws in how an application enforces its own business rules, such as workflow bypass, race conditions, price manipulation, and automated abuse.

Successful candidates can:

- Identify and prevent race conditions and TOCTOU vulnerabilities
- Implement secure multi-step workflows resistant to bypass attacks
- Apply anti-automation controls and business rule validation
- Prevent abuse scenarios that can be generated with lack of authorization checks. Such as price manipulation, cart tampering

---

## Expected competency
Candidates can implement business logic securely to ensure vulnerabilities are addressed with business rule validation and access controls.
