# Objectives for Module 11 – Business Logic Security

This module covers flaws in the rules and workflows that define what an application is allowed to do, and how developers recognize and limit their abuse.
These flaws let an attacker misuse legitimate functions, such as changing a price, skipping a payment step, or repeating a one-time offer.

A successful candidate can:

- Identify business logic flaws and distinguish them from implementation defects such as injection
- Recognize workflow and state-transition weaknesses that allow steps to be skipped, repeated, or reordered
- Identify race conditions and time-of-check to time-of-use (TOCTOU) flaws and the controls that make a check and its action atomic
- Recognize how trusting client-supplied values enables price, quantity, and discount manipulation
- Identify business rules that the server must validate, including limits, boundary values, and cross-field constraints
- Distinguish anti-automation controls and recognize their limits against abuse of legitimate functions
- Identify system behavior that fails securely when a business rule or a dependency fails
- Recognize abuse-case and boundary-value tests that expose business logic flaws

## Expected competency

Candidates can review a feature such as checkout, account recovery, or coupon redemption.
They can identify the business rules that need server-side enforcement and the points where an attacker could skip, repeat, reorder, or race a step.
They can recognize which controls and tests address each weakness.
