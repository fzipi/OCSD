# Objectives for Module 15 - Security of and for AI

This module covers the security risks of building applications that use large language models (LLMs) and the controls a developer applies to reduce them.

A successful candidate can:

- Identify and mitigate OWASP LLM Top 10 (2025) vulnerabilities
- Implement defenses against prompt injection and system prompt leakage
- Secure vector databases and RAG implementations against embedding attacks
- Apply controls for excessive agency, unbounded consumption, and misinformation risks
- Design guardrails and content filtering for AI-generated outputs
- Understand general architecture of modern AI systems
- Understand how to perform basic checks during development and testing of AI systems

---

## Expected competency
Candidates can recognize the main security risks of LLM-based features and identify the controls that reduce them in a realistic development context.
