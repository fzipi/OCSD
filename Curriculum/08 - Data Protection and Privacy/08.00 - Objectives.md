# Objectives for Module 8 - Data Protection and Privacy

This module covers how applications classify, store, share, retain, and delete sensitive and personal data, and how privacy principles and laws shape those decisions.

A successful candidate can:

- Distinguish data classification levels and recognize the handling controls each level requires
- Identify how encryption at rest, encryption in transit, and separate key storage protect sensitive data, and what they do not protect against
- Recognize data minimization, purpose limitation, and retention practices that reduce risk
- Distinguish anonymization from pseudonymization and identify the techniques and limits of each
- Distinguish data deletion methods and identify where deleted data can persist
- Recognize the principles of privacy by design and by default and how they affect application design
- Identify common privacy law requirements that become technical controls
- Identify checks that verify data protection controls

## Expected competency

Candidates can recognize which data in an application is sensitive or personal and which protections it needs.
They can identify when a design collects, keeps, shares, or exposes more data than necessary.
They can tell whether a proposed technique, such as hashing, masking, or deletion, achieves the privacy goal claimed for it.
